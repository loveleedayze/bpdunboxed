What Is BPD Unboxed?

I'm here to unbox the myths about Borderline Personality Disorder and replace them with neuroscience, truth, and real tools for nervous system regulation. No shame. No softening. Just the work.

My mission: to build a community of people who feel intensely, heal intentionally, and stop apologizing for their depth.
